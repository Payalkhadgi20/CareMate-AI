
# CareMate AI – Healthcare Appointment Assistant

## 1. Product Overview
CareMate AI is an AI-powered healthcare appointment assistant designed to help users discover suitable doctors, explore medical specialities, and navigate the appointment booking process.

## 2. Problem Statement
People often face difficulties identifying the right medical speciality, finding suitable doctors, and checking appointment availability.

CareMate AI aims to simplify this process through a conversational and user-friendly experience.

## 3. Target Users
- People looking for suitable doctors.
- Users unfamiliar with medical specialities.
- People who find appointment booking confusing or time-consuming.

## 4. Key Features
- Conversational appointment assistance.
- Speciality exploration and suggestions.
- Doctor discovery.
- Doctor profile exploration.
- Appointment booking guidance.
- Appointment status and next-step information.

## 5. AI Capabilities and Limitations
- Understand natural-language appointment requests.
- Ask clarifying questions when information is incomplete.
- Suggest relevant medical specialities without diagnosing.
- Provide reliable doctor and appointment information when connected to verified data sources.
- Not intended to replace professional medical advice or diagnosis.

## 6. Product Development
This project includes:
- Product Requirements Document (PRD).
- User research and survey findings.
- Figma prototype.
- Usability testing.
- Product case study.

## 7. Project Objective
To demonstrate practical AI Product Management skills, including product discovery, user research, requirements definition, AI behaviour design, prototyping, and usability testing.

## 8. Current Status
Portfolio prototype and product documentation.
