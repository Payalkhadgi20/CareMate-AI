
# CareMate AI – Product Overview

## 1. Product Vision
To simplify healthcare appointment discovery and booking through an AI-powered conversational assistant.

## 2. Problem Statement
Users often face difficulties identifying the right medical speciality, finding suitable doctors, and checking appointment availability.

## 3. Proposed Solution
CareMate AI helps users through a conversational experience to:
- Explore medical specialities.
- Discover suitable doctors.
- View appointment availability when connected to reliable data sources.
- Navigate the booking process.

## 4. Target Users
- People looking for suitable doctors.
- Users unfamiliar with medical specialities.
- People who find appointment booking confusing.

## 5. Key Features
- Conversational appointment assistance.
- Speciality suggestions.
- Doctor discovery.
- Doctor profile exploration.
- Appointment booking guidance.

## 6. AI Limitations
- Does not diagnose medical conditions.
- Does not prescribe medicines.
- Does not replace professional medical advice.

## 7. Project Components
- Product Requirements Document (PRD).
- User research.
- Figma prototype.
- Usability testing.
- Product case study.

## 8. Project Status
Portfolio prototype and product documentation.
